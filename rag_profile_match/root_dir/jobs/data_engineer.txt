Senior Data Engineer
Company: Logistics data platform | Location: Bangalore, India (hybrid)

About the role
We're looking for a Senior Data Engineer to build and operate the
pipelines that power our analytics and reporting products.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain batch and streaming data pipelines in Python.
- Orchestrate pipelines with Airflow and process large datasets with
  Spark.
- Model data for a cloud data warehouse and support analyst self-service.
- Partner with data scientists and analysts on data availability and
  quality.

Must-have requirements
- 5+ years of data engineering experience.
- 5+ years of hands-on Python development.
- Experience with SQL and a cloud data warehouse (Redshift, Snowflake, or
  BigQuery).
- Experience with a workflow orchestration tool (Airflow or similar).

Nice-to-have
- Experience with Apache Spark.
- Experience with dbt for data transformation.
- Experience with streaming systems (Kafka or similar).
