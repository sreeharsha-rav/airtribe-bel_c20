Senior Product Designer
Company: Healthcare fintech | Location: Bangalore, India (hybrid)

About the role
We're hiring a Senior Product Designer to lead design for our core
product experience, from research through shipped, polished UI.

Responsibilities
- Own end-to-end product design for major features, from early concepts
  to production-ready specs in Figma.
- Build and evolve our design system in partnership with engineering.
- Run or guide usability research to validate design decisions.
- Mentor other designers and raise the bar for design craft.

Must-have requirements
- 6+ years of product design experience.
- Strong portfolio of shipped product design work in Figma.
- Experience building or scaling a design system.
- Experience mentoring or leading other designers.

Nice-to-have
- Experience in fintech, healthcare, or another regulated industry.
- Experience running usability testing or user research.
