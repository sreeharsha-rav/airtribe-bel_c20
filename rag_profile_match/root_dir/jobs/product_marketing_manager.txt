Product Marketing Manager
Company: B2B SaaS platform | Location: Remote (EU time zones)

About the role
We're hiring a Product Marketing Manager to own go-to-market strategy and
positioning for our core product.

Responsibilities
- Develop positioning and messaging for new features and product lines.
- Lead go-to-market planning for product launches, in partnership with
  product and sales.
- Run competitive analysis to inform product and marketing strategy.
- Build sales enablement content that helps reps sell more effectively.

Must-have requirements
- 5+ years of product marketing experience at a B2B SaaS company.
- Demonstrated experience owning go-to-market strategy for product
  launches.
- Experience developing positioning and messaging.

Nice-to-have
- Experience building sales enablement programs.
- Experience partnering directly with product management.
