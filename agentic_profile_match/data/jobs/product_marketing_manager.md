Product Marketing Manager
Company: Fernwood Software | Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

About the role
We're hiring a Product Marketing Manager to own go-to-market for our B2B
SaaS platform. You'll partner closely with product and sales to bring new
features to market and keep our positioning sharp.

Responsibilities
- Lead go-to-market planning and execution for new product launches.
- Write positioning and messaging documents used across sales and
  marketing.
- Partner with sales to build enablement materials and support the sales
  cycle.
- Run competitive research to inform roadmap and pricing decisions.

Must-have requirements
- 4+ years of product marketing or product management experience.
- Experience launching B2B SaaS products.
- Experience writing positioning and messaging documents.
- Experience collaborating with sales and product teams.

Nice-to-have
- Experience with marketing analytics tools.
- Experience with competitive research programs.
- MBA or related business degree.
